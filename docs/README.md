# OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, cross-functional approach to project management that moves from initiation to planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Every initiative begins with a clear problem statement, stakeholder alignment, and a one-pager that defines success metrics and the path to approval. This keeps teams focused on customer value, shared outcomes, and a common understanding of scope before work expands into delivery. The result is a practical model for project management that is easy to adopt, track, and explain to new team members.

The planning phase turns approved work into a prioritized backlog, milestones, and acceptance criteria. Teams define the Definition of Done, identify dependencies and risks, and create a release plan that respects capacity and cross-team needs. During execution, the team works through daily standups, weekly reviews, demos, and a project board that makes progress visible from backlog to QA to done. This rhythm keeps work transparent while giving the team room to adapt as new information or blockers emerge.

OctoAcme relies on clear roles and personas to keep delivery aligned. Product leaders define outcomes and priorities, project managers coordinate schedules and communication, developers build and test solutions, and QA validates quality against acceptance criteria. Communication is regular and deliberate, with weekly updates, stakeholder briefings, escalation paths for blockers, and a single source of truth for status and decisions. Risk management is treated as part of the normal delivery flow so issues are surfaced early and addressed before they disrupt milestones or customer experience.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout the lifecycle rather than reserved for the end. Teams use automated tests, CI checks, smoke tests, security scanning, and manual QA where needed to confirm work meets agreed standards before release. Retrospectives close the loop by capturing what went well, what should improve, and which actions should move into the next sprint or release. That continuous feedback cycle keeps OctoAcme’s project management approach practical, scalable, and focused on value delivery.
