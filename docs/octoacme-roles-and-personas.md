# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Engineers

### Role Summary
QA Engineers ensure that delivered work meets acceptance criteria, releases are safe, and quality is treated as a shared responsibility across the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and regression coverage for key functionality
- Validate features against acceptance criteria and user workflows
- Identify, document, and triage defects alongside developers and PMs
- Support release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Partner with product and engineering to improve testability

### Goals
- Catch issues before release and reduce customer-facing defects
- Improve confidence in changes across each iteration
- Maintain a consistent quality bar for delivery

### Typical Communication
- Test status during standups and sprint reviews
- Defect triage and regression reports
- Release readiness checklists and QA sign-off notes

---

## Engineering Leads / Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction, maintain architectural integrity, and help the team deliver work in a sustainable way while managing dependencies and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and design standards for the project
- Review architecture, implementation choices, and technical risks
- Help break down work into manageable increments and dependencies
- Mentor engineers and support technical decision-making
- Coordinate with PMs and Product Leads on delivery impact and sequencing

### Goals
- Keep solutions maintainable, testable, and aligned with platform standards
- Unblock delivery without compromising engineering quality
- Balance speed, stability, and technical sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical planning sessions
- Architecture and dependency updates in team syncs
- Escalations for cross-team integration or technical risk

---

## Security & Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and compliance partners protect the project from avoidable risk by ensuring security, privacy, and regulatory requirements are considered early and continuously.

### Responsibilities
- Review high-risk changes, dependencies, and data handling practices
- Support threat modeling, access controls, and secure deployment practices
- Validate compliance requirements relevant to the project
- Coordinate response to security incidents or escalations
- Help teams understand risk trade-offs and remediation paths

### Goals
- Reduce security exposure and operational risk
- Keep customer trust and regulatory commitments intact
- Make security a continuous part of delivery, not a late-stage gate

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat-modeling workshops
- Incident communications and follow-up action plans
- Compliance checkpoints tied to release and deployment work

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads connect delivery work to real customer outcomes, feedback, and operational readiness. They help translate usage patterns into product and project improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, pain points, and adoption trends
- Support rollout readiness, training, and support documentation
- Track issues that require escalation or follow-up after release
- Share customer impact data with Product and Project Managers
- Help prioritize fixes based on business and customer urgency

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce time to value for new releases and improvements
- Keep the roadmap grounded in user experience and support signals

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops and issue triage
- Launch readiness updates and support handoff notes
- Release notes, adoption updates, and stakeholder summaries

---

## Sponsors / Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors and stakeholders provide the business context, approvals, and strategic priorities that keep the project aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm priority, budget, and business case for the initiative
- Review progress against milestones and strategic outcomes
- Resolve ambiguities on scope, timing, and cross-functional dependencies
- Provide approvals for major decisions and release gates
- Champion successful adoption of the final solution

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned to business value and strategy
- Support timely decisions and resource availability
- Create accountability for measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Steering reviews and milestone briefings
- Decision logs and executive status updates
- Business-impact summaries and release approval discussions
